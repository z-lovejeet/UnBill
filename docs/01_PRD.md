# Product requirements

Current release: 26 October 2026. The authoritative requirements are `devpost/prd.md`, with scope in `devpost/scope.md`.

UnBill helps a person inspect itemized medical charges and prepare a billing-team review request. Supported flows are document/text intake, explicit provider consent, validated structured extraction, limited heuristic findings, selected-item reconciliation, a matching letter and phone guide, optional streaming coach, encrypted device history and revocable redacted sharing.

Success means the report is internally consistent, unknown facts stay unknown, unavailable IDs never become sample records, failures are recoverable, and the product's claims match its actual scope. The public demo uses synthetic/de-identified data. It does not establish clinical appropriateness, fraud, enforceable legal protections, official prices or guaranteed savings.

See `devpost/checklist.md` and `docs/audits/2026-10-26/RELEASE.md` for verification and external shipping status. Historical audit files are not current pass/fail evidence.
