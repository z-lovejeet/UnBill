## Recording and submission

The contributor is recording the video and handling Devpost. The repository documentation and recording materials are prepared. A final public video URL, personal form confirmations, exit survey and Devpost submission receipt are external steps, tracked in [shipping status](shipping-status.md).

The demonstration uses synthetic/de-identified bills. The 19-code comparison catalog is illustrative, and findings are questions for review rather than confirmed billing violations or savings.
