UnBill: Forensic Hospital Bill Auditor & Statutory Dispute Engine

A deterministic, multi-agent patient advocacy platform designed to decode cryptographic hospital CPT codes, detect duplicate and unbundled charges against Medicare benchmarks, and compile formal Fair Credit Billing Act (15 U.S.C. § 1666) dispute packages.

The Core Architecture: 4 Specialized Agents

UnBill replaces brittle probabilistic AI loops with a hybrid sequential engine: multimodal vision for intake, deterministic rules for math and statutory NCCI compliance, and ultra-fast LLMs for patient coaching.

Architectural Code Tour: Key Source Locations

Stop 1: Multimodal Vision Ingestion & Schema Sanitization

src/lib/gemini.ts → extractBillFromImage() and extractBillFromText()

Receives user statements, applies structured JSON instructions, and returns standardized clinical rows containing CPT code, raw description, date, and billed amounts.

Stop 2: Zero-Hallucination Deterministic Forensic Rule Engine

src/lib/rules-engine.ts → compileForensicAudit()

Executes exact mathematical checks against data/cpt_benchmarks.json:

  • auditDuplicateCharges(): flags duplicate line items billed on identical dates.
  • auditUnbundling(): identifies CMS National Correct Coding Initiative (NCCI) component violations.
  • auditExcessiveMarkups(): compares hospital charges against regional Medicare Median Allowables.

Stop 3: Interactive Forensic Dashboard & Dispute Kit UI

src/app/results/[id]/page.tsx & src/components/audit-table.tsx

Renders the clinical ledger, plain-English drawer explanations, benchmark comparison visualizers, and dynamic savings recalculation as line items are toggled.

Stop 4: Certified Legal Letter & Phone Negotiation Scripts

src/app/dispute/[id]/page.tsx

Compiles a formal Fair Credit Billing Act notice with USPS Certified Mail tracking fields, and provides 4 interactive phone negotiation scripts with one-click copy buttons.

Stop 5: Ultra-Low Latency Conversational Copilot

src/app/api/chat/route.ts & src/components/copilot-drawer.tsx

Streams Groq responses via Server-Sent Events with sub-350ms first-token latency, automatically injected with the patient's active audit context.

To Change This, Start Here

  • Add or update clinical procedures & Medicare benchmarks: Edit data/cpt_benchmarks.json.
  • Update federal consumer statutory grounds: Edit data/legal_statutes.json.
  • Modify LLM models or temperatures: Update src/lib/gemini.ts (for vision extraction) or src/app/api/chat/route.ts (for Groq Copilot).
  • Adjust UI theme or clinical color tokens: Edit tailwind.config.ts and src/app/globals.css.

A Practice to Reuse: The Hybrid Deterministic AI Pattern

Medical billing and consumer law have zero tolerance for mathematical hallucinations. A critical design decision in UnBill is isolating AI to perception and communication (Gemini vision OCR for document reading and Groq for conversational coaching), while reserving critical business calculations, duplicate detection, and statutory compliance entirely for deterministic TypeScript algorithms.

This hybrid architecture guarantees 100% computational correctness, reproducible benchmark comparisons, and verifiable legal citations while leveraging the natural language power of modern LLMs.
